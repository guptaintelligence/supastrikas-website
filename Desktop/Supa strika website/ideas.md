# Supastrikas Clothing Recreation — Reference-Led Design Specification

## Ground-Truth Reference

This project is a faithful recreation of the supplied homepage at `https://supastrikas-clothing.lovable.app/`. Fidelity to the source hierarchy, visual language, content rhythm, and interaction cues overrides other stylistic exploration.

The reference is a single-page, limited-drop streetwear preorder page built around the Supa Strikas football universe. Its core visual system uses **near-black, signal red, saturated yellow, and paper white** with a deliberately raw print-poster treatment: thick rules, diagonal hazard-stripe separators, dense condensed type, block labels, cutout product photography, and hard black offset shadows.

## Observed Page Structure

1. A black utility header with a logo mark at left, a yellow outlined preorder button at right, and a thin yellow/red band below.
2. A tall red hero with an oversized `THE FIRST DROP.` headline at bottom left, countdown clock, preorder CTA, price/free-shipping callout, and a jersey image anchoring the lower-right portion.
3. A black booking-details strip with release-date messaging, social controls, and an overlaid yellow/red band.
4. A black jersey showcase with `THE JERSEY.` heading, a front/back selector, and two large red-backed product image panels.
5. A bright yellow pricing panel with the $85 price, availability bar, and red reserve button with offset black shadow.
6. A paper-white details area with an asymmetric heading and six black feature tiles, followed by a three-step ordering sequence.
7. A black reservation block containing the preorder form, yellow/red supporting details, and an outlined/filled size selector.
8. A warm paper-white tribute section with large stacked editorial writing, three value cards, and a final preorder CTA.
9. A compact black footer with the logo mark, brand line, social icons, and small-print legal copy.

## Exacting Style Rules

**Design Movement:** Street-football fanzine / 2000s sports broadcast graphic design.

**Core Principles:** Use dramatic contrast over subtlety; make typographic blocks feel printed and slightly imperfect; prefer hard-edged panels and offset shadows to rounded UI; retain clear visual alternation between red, black, yellow, and white sections.

**Color Philosophy:** Red communicates team energy and launch urgency; yellow acts as a bright editorial highlighter and CTA signal; black provides weight and football-night atmosphere; off-white gives the reading sections a poster-stock texture. The palette must remain flat and assertive rather than gradient-driven.

**Layout Paradigm:** Long-form editorial strip with strong horizontal bands and deliberately uneven content alignment. Individual sections should read as pages pasted from a limited-run matchday zine, not as generic centered marketing cards.

**Signature Elements:** Yellow/red double rules; black diagonal hatch bands; high-contrast ticker-like labels; 3–5px hard offset shadows; narrow uppercase display headlines; number-led preorder steps.

**Interaction Philosophy:** CTAs use concise action language, anchor-scroll to the booking form, and depress on click. The front/back showcase and size options clearly telegraph state changes without visual gloss.

**Animation:** Keep movement restrained. CTA press responses occur in under 160ms. Hovering product panels should make the product image rise by a few pixels; section entrances can use a light opacity/translate reveal respecting reduced-motion settings.

**Typography System:** Use a condensed athletic display family for headings and labels (Anton / Archivo Narrow fallbacks) plus a neutral grotesk for explanatory text (Barlow / Arial fallbacks). Headlines are uppercase and densely tracked; body copy is compact but readable.

**Brand Essence:** A streetwear jersey preorder for fans who grew up treating Supa Strikas as football culture, not just a cartoon. Personality: **kinetic, nostalgic, unapologetic**.

**Brand Voice:** Headlines are short, declarative, and rhythmic; CTAs are direct and action-first; supporting copy is community-minded but no-nonsense. Example: `BUILT FOR THE PITCH. READY FOR THE BLOCK.` and `LOCK IN THE DROP.`

**Wordmark & Logo:** Retain the supplied reference mark treatment where available. The brand symbol is a sharp, badge-adjacent graphic with yellow/red/black sports iconography, never a plain default font wordmark.

**Signature Brand Color:** Drop Yellow — `#FFD719`.

## Inspection Notes

The first two reference scrolls confirmed the top portion uses a vivid solid-red hero and an all-black jersey gallery with wide product panels. The pricing section becomes a solid yellow field, centering a very large black price, a thin availability meter, and a red hard-shadowed reservation button. The first product image panel displays a red jersey cutout over black, while the image asset itself may be contained in a red-background photo.
